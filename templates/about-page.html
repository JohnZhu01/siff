About the Festival

The Seaside Independent Film Festival (SIFF) celebrates independent filmmaking by bringing unique stories, creative voices, and film lovers together.

Our goal is to create a welcoming space for filmmakers to share their work, discover fresh perspectives, and connect with the local community.

By showcasing diverse films and supporting emerging talent, we aim to make independent cinema accessible and inspire a love for storytelling on the Sunshine Coast.
