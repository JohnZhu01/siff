Welcome To SIFF

June 11–13, 2027 | Gibsons, British Columbia

Where Independent Stories Come to Life

Experience three days of independent cinema, creative storytelling, and community on British Columbia’s beautiful Sunshine Coast.

The Seaside Independent Film Festival (SIFF) brings together emerging filmmakers, passionate storytellers, and film enthusiasts to celebrate the art of independent filmmaking. From captivating feature films and original short films to engaging workshops and filmmaker discussions, SIFF offers something for everyone.

Join us in Gibsons for a weekend of inspiring stories, new perspectives, and unforgettable cinematic experiences.
